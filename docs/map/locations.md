# Location names and map coverage

Each label links to its originating community source. The local atlas preserves original Chinese wording where an independently verified English name is unavailable.

## What this guide can and cannot establish

Search the local atlas for a source label, habitat phrase, or linked creature form. Habitat results and handbook areas expose their associated local Dex rows in the atlas. Counts describe source-index relationships, not geographic area size, number of spawn points, encounter probability, or live availability.

The source files provide no verified local coordinates, polygons, route lines, map tiles, or external viewer point IDs. No complete Season 4 access map or unlock table was found. Do not infer travel routes, access requirements, encounter rates, current availability, or a complete world boundary from these labels.

The [official Season 4 announcement](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/847022426351994538?group_id=228326) confirms the Moon surface as a seasonal destination; it does not establish a complete coordinate atlas or access table. Check the current game map and in-game prompts for live access details.

## Data fields and reuse

Each S3 label, habitat phrase, and handbook area in `dist/data/locations.json` carries its source URL, source date, license, and record-specific uncertainty. Habitat phrases and handbook areas retain their linked local record IDs. The root fields retain the checked date, snapshot identity, scope, and completeness statement.
