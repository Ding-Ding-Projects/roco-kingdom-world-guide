# Creature roster counts and unresolved form keys

## What each count measures

This edition's saved creature snapshot, reviewed 2026-09-24, contains 625 form rows across 466 catalog numbers. The current BiliWiki `精灵列表` page displayed 621 results and showed an update date of 2026-09-13 when checked on 2026-09-25. The independent Roco Kingdom World creature index reported 644 entries on 2026-09-25.

These are community indexes with different snapshot dates, record shapes, and visible numbering systems. No source establishes that the numbers count the same units. None is an official export or a confirmed total of every creature currently available.

## Local keys and unresolved matches

The saved local data includes form keys `047-02`, `048-02`, `048-04`, and `162-02`. Comparing names and displayed keys still leaves differences, including family and form labels. The comparison did not establish an exact identity for every row across all three indexes.

Keep the local records and the mismatch note. Do not subtract the totals to claim that creatures are missing, remove a form because another index does not show its key, or add a guessed match. A future data refresh needs a reusable source with stable identifiers and a reviewed row-level mapping.

## How to verify a record

Search by name or catalog number in the guide, open the form detail, and follow its source links. Check the form label and the source update date rather than relying on display order. For current unlock requirements, live values, or present availability, confirm against the current client and a dated official notice.
