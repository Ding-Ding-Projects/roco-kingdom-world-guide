The live guide stores article text and references in `dist/data/articles.json` and source records in `dist/data/sources.json`. Each article has English and written-Cantonese content, dated sources, a confidence note, and explicit limits. External source links open in a new tab with `noopener noreferrer`; references to another guide article remain internal links. Markdown exports preserve both kinds of reference.

- [Roster count comparison and unresolved form keys](roster-counts.md)

## Editorial rules

- Official notices support only the dates, requirements, system names, and rewards they state.
- Community data supports names, lookup coverage, and historical snapshots. It does not establish official completeness or current live-game rules.
- Exact encounter odds, currency conversions, breeding claims, and team builds remain patch-sensitive unless a dated primary source confirms them.
- The September 24 patch summary and some weekly checklist details were image-led and not readable in the reviewed text. Unreadable claims are not summarized as fact.
- Verify event timers and eligibility in the live client, especially when an event is near its end date.

## Rendering and privacy

The article view renders bundled JSON and escapes dynamic text. External source links are not embedded or proxied. Search and saved notes run locally in the current browser. If a source changes or disappears, keep the edition date beside the claim and mark it for review rather than silently replacing it with an unverified summary.
