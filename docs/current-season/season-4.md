# Season 4 and September 2026 notices

## Verified schedule and systems

The official season schedule lists the S4 window as 2026-09-10 04:00 to 2026-11-05 04:00.
Confirm timezone context using the game client before setting a deadline. Official notes
name the Moon surface, Visitor from Beyond assignment, 银月狼王, Starfall Light,
eight new creature families, and 16 season-variant families. These broad names do not expose
every route, encounter trigger, or eligible form.

The [September 24 update notice](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/851922582704753293?group_id=228326)
was found, but its image details could not be reliably read during source review. The guide
does not claim a complete summary of that update.

## Events near this edition date

The official forum index announced the Cocoa Harvest Festival for September 25 and advertised
up to 480 free Cocoa Fruit Balls across the event. Its extracted text did not establish the
event end date or every reward condition. Confirm the live event page before budgeting around
the advertised maximum.

The official Destined Flower Seed notice states Magic Level 40 and Spirit Scholar rank
requirements, with a September 10 to September 25 04:00 window. At this edition date, that
window is close to ending. Verify the in-game timer and current notice.

## Failure and freshness limits

Do not treat the S4 window as the availability window for every linked activity. Do not
project a date's reset timezone from an event post. Do not reuse old season conversion, pity,
breeding, catch-rate, or shop-stock figures as current.
