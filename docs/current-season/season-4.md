## Official event notices checked September 26

### Cocoa Harvest Festival

The [official September 25 preview](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/849231723693606703), checked 2026-09-26, says each player can receive up to 480 free Cocoa Fruit Balls during the festival. Using a ball to catch a creature during the event is stated to trigger the Starfall encounter, 星陨流光.

The readable notice does not state any upgrade details, claim requirements, delivery schedule, event end date, or time zone. The referenced post image could not be verified in this review, so upgrade quantities beyond the readable official text are omitted. The 480 is a maximum across the event, not proof that all balls are immediately available. Check the live event panel for claim steps and expiry. Encounter odds are not provided by this notice.

### Weekend appearance: 刺轮砣

The [official weekend notice](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/852303601920379188), checked 2026-09-26, lists the event from 2026-09-25 04:00 through 2026-09-28 04:00. It does not state a time zone. Eligibility is Magic Level 20 and the rank 见习魔法生. The notice says the 刺轮砣 family appears in large numbers and that the two shiny appearances, 上弦 and 下弦, may be encountered. “May” is not a guarantee, and the notice gives no odds. Check the in-game timer and event panel.
