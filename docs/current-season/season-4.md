# Season 4 and September 2026 notices

## Verified schedule and systems

The official season schedule lists the S4 window as 2026-09-10 04:00 to 2026-11-05 04:00.
Confirm timezone context using the game client before setting a deadline. Official notes
name the Moon surface, Visitor from Beyond assignment, 银月狼王, Starfall Light,
eight new creature families, and 16 season-variant families. These broad names do not expose
every route, encounter trigger, or eligible form.

The [September 24 update notice](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/851922582704753293?group_id=228326)
was found, but its image details could not be reliably read during source review. The guide
does not claim a complete summary of that update.

## Event windows checked on September 25

### Cocoa Harvest Festival

The [official September 25 preview](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/849231723693606703) says each
player can receive up to 480 free Cocoa Fruit Balls across the event. Using a ball to catch a
creature during the event is stated to trigger the Starfall encounter, 星陨流光. The readable
post does not give the end date, delivery pace, or every reward condition. Treat 480 as a
cumulative maximum, not an immediate inventory count. Check the in-game event, mail, and merchant
pages for the claim route and expiry before planning catches.

### Destined Flower Seed

The [community event archive](https://wiki.biligame.com/nrc/%E5%91%BD%E5%AE%9A%E8%8A%B1%E7%A7%8D%EF%BC%882026-09-25%EF%BC%89)
lists the next cycle from 2026-09-25 04:00 through 2026-10-09 03:59 China Standard Time
(UTC+08:00). The readable entry labels participation requirements as “未解锁” (not unlocked).
Its linked infographic could not be transcribed in this review, so the next-cycle roster, access
requirements, repeat limits, and rewards remain unverified. This is a community archive, not an
official notice. Check the pinned challenge and its live rules before building a team.

The prior [official notice](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/847120033397081827?group_id=228326)
covers the September 10 phase only: it named 寂灭骨龙（固执）, 星星眼（沉默）, and 智辉章脑（胆小）,
and required Magic Level 40 and Spirit Scholar rank. Successful challenges awarded the matching
creature and first-clear rewards including 400 分光水晶 and 灵魂环印. It also describes a
“命定勇者” medal for winning solo with one creature, one real player, no creature swap, and no
exhaustion. These rules and rewards are not confirmed for the September 25 cycle.

### Pika Photography Commission

The [official notice](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/847117678085670466) lists the commission
from the September 10 update through 2026-09-25 04:00. The extracted post does not state a time
zone, and the cutoff falls on this edition date. Check the live client timer rather than assume
the commission is open or closed. Do not plan around the previous target list; a later window is
not confirmed by this notice.

## Failure and freshness limits

Do not treat the S4 window as the availability window for every linked activity. Do not
project a date's reset timezone from an event post. Do not reuse old season conversion, pity,
breeding, catch-rate, or shop-stock figures as current.
